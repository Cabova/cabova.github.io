Zero-Data Collection Policy

Privacy Policy for Chop & Crunch

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Developer: Cabova Games / Cabova Digital

Cabova Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information for our casual physics puzzle mobile application, Chop & Crunch ("the Game"), available on Android and iOS.

Core Guarantee: Chop & Crunch is built on a 100% "Local-First" architecture. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data from our players.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because the Game runs entirely locally on your device without an account requirement, we do not collect any personal information:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials.
  • No Location Data: We do not collect GPS coordinates, IP locations, or Wi-Fi network information.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect or track IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or personal advertising IDs.
  • No Financial / Payment Data: The game is free to play and does not process or collect credit card or banking information.

2. Local Game Data & Storage

All gameplay data — including unlocked levels, high scores, earned culinary stars, and player settings (such as sound effects and music volume) — is saved strictly locally on your device's internal application storage.

  • This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by clearing the app's cache/data via your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The initial release of Chop & Crunch does not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. You can enjoy uninterrupted gameplay completely offline without network tracking.

4. Device Permissions

Chop & Crunch requests only the minimal runtime system capabilities necessary for local game execution:

  • Audio Output: To play background music and sound effects.
  • Haptic / Vibration Feedback: To provide tactile sensations when slicing vegetables or ricocheting off paddles (can be toggled in settings).

We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, storage files, or device sensors.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Chop & Crunch is designed as a family-friendly casual game suitable for players of all ages:

  • We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Because no personal data is collected from any player, no children's data is ever gathered, retained, or shared.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if future updates introduce online capabilities (such as cloud saves or leaderboards). Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
